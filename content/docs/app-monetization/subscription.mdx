## Introduction

Subscriptions trade one-time payments for predictable, compounding revenue. A thousand users paying $2/month is $24,000 a year — and it renews automatically. This model rewards apps that deliver ongoing value.

## Why subscriptions win

```text
One-time sale:   $5 × 1 purchase = $5 lifetime (per user)
Subscription:    $2 × 12 months  = $24 lifetime (per retained user)
```

The math compounds further because subscribers who stay are your most engaged users.

## Choosing what to charge for

Good subscription candidates:

- Ongoing content (courses, tips, reports)
- Ongoing tools (cloud sync, unlimited storage, AI features)
- Removal of ads + premium experience

Poor candidates: one-time utility features (a calculator doesn't need a monthly plan).

## Pricing structure

| Plan | Typical price | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $1.99–$4.99 | Low commitment entry |
| Yearly | ~40–60% of monthly×12 | Best value — push this |
| Free trial | 3–7 days | Doubles conversion |

<Callout type="tip" title="Make yearly the default">
Play Billing lets you mark one plan as the default selection. Apps that pre-select the yearly plan see significantly higher annual revenue per subscriber.
</Callout>

## Implementing with Play Billing

Flutter's `in_app_purchase` package covers both IAP and subscriptions:

```dart
// Query available subscription plans
final response = await InAppPurchase.instance
    .queryProductDetails({'premium_monthly', 'premium_yearly'});

// Purchase
final param = PurchaseParam(productDetails: product);
await InAppPurchase.instance.buyNonConsumable(
  purchaseParam: param,
);
```

Then listen to the purchase stream and validate receipts server-side when possible.

## The paywall that converts

1. **Context first**: show the paywall at the moment of value ("Export this report? Go Premium").
2. **List benefits**, not features: "Unlimited exports" beats "Pro plan enabled".
3. **Social proof and clarity**: ratings, no-hidden-fees line.
4. **Restore purchases button** — required by policy on iOS, expected everywhere.

## Retention is everything

Churn kills subscription businesses. Reduce it with:

- Onboarding emails/notifications during trial
- Regular new content or feature drops
- Win-back offers on cancel screens
