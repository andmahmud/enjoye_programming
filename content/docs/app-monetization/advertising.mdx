## Introduction

Advertising is the default business model of the free internet — and of free apps. You give users something valuable at no cost; advertisers pay to reach those users through you. Done well, users barely notice the ads and revenue compounds with growth.

## How ad revenue is calculated

Two numbers dominate every dashboard:

- **eCPM** (effective cost per mille) — what you earn per 1,000 *impressions*:

```text
eCPM = (Total earnings ÷ Impressions) × 1,000
```

- **RPM** (revenue per mille) — earnings per 1,000 app *sessions* or screens.

```dart
// Daily ad revenue example
final dailyImpressions = dailyUsers * adsPerUser;
final dailyRevenue = dailyImpressions * ecpm / 1000;
```

Try your own numbers in the [App Income Calculator](/tools/app-income-calculator).

## What moves eCPM

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| User country | US/UK/DE eCPMs can be 5–10× South Asian rates |
| Ad format | Rewarded > Interstitial > Native > Banner |
| Season | Q4 (holidays) peaks; January dips |
| Niche | Finance & shopping ads pay more than games |

## The format playbook

1. **Banner** on content-heavy screens — steady baseline.
2. **Interstitial** after completed actions — volume spikes.
3. **Rewarded** where users want value — highest eCPM.
4. **App Open** at cold start — free launch-time money.
5. **Native** inside feeds when design matters.

Full implementation guides live under [AdMob](/docs/admob/getting-started).

<Callout type="warning" title="More ads ≠ more money">
Every extra ad costs retention. Losing a daily-active user usually costs more than one additional impression earns. Optimize *revenue per user over time*, not today's impressions.
</Callout>

## Measuring what matters

Track weekly:

- eCPM per format
- Sessions per user (retention proxy)
- Revenue per user (ARPU)
- Crash-free sessions (ad SDKs can hurt stability)
