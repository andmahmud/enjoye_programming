## Introduction

Ads, subscriptions and IAP are only part of the map. Some of the most profitable "apps" earn through affiliate commissions, service fees or selling digital products. This page maps the wider business landscape so you can pick — or stack — the right models.

## Affiliate marketing

Promote relevant products inside your app; earn a commission per sale or signup.

| App niche | Affiliate fit |
| --- | --- |
| Finance / budgeting | Broker & card referrals |
| Education | Courses, books |
| Health | Supplements, gear |
| Travel | Hotels, insurance |

Rules of thumb:

- Only promote things you'd recommend anyway — trust is the asset.
- Disclose affiliate links (required by law in many countries).
- Track with deep links + UTM parameters.

## Service business model

The app is the front door of a service:

```text
Client finds your app → Books/orders → You deliver → You charge
```

Examples: laundry booking, tutoring marketplace, freelancer client portals. Revenue comes from service fees, not ad impressions. One paying client can be worth thousands of banner clicks.

<Callout type="tip" title="Local niches are underrated">
A simple booking app for one city's car-wash businesses can out-earn a global game — because each customer is worth real money monthly.
</Callout>

## Digital products

Sell what you create while building apps:

- Flutter UI kits and templates
- Icon packs, presets, fonts
- Courses and e-books teaching what you learned

Distribution: your own app, Gumroad, Play Store listings, or your YouTube audience. Margins are near 100% after creation.

## Stacking models responsibly

A healthy app often combines 2–3 streams:

| Stream | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Ads | Monetizes the free majority |
| Premium/Subscription | Converts engaged users |
| Affiliate/Digital | Extracts value from high-intent users |

Keep each stream policy-compliant ([Policy Compliance](/docs/play-store/policy-compliance)) and never let monetization degrade core UX — retention funds everything else.

## Choosing by goal

- **Maximize downloads** → ads-only, zero friction
- **Maximize revenue per user** → subscription-led
- **Build a brand** → content + digital products
- **Serve local market** → service model
