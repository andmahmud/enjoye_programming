## Introduction

Every app that collects *any* data — even just an advertising ID from AdMob — must publish a privacy policy, both inside the app and as a public URL in Play Console. The good news: for most apps this is a one-page document you can write in an hour.

## What your policy must state

1. **Who you are** — developer/company name and contact email.
2. **What data is collected** — be specific:
   - Advertising ID / device identifiers (AdMob)
   - Analytics events (Firebase)
   - Email address if you use authentication
   - Crash logs
3. **Why it's collected** — ads personalization, analytics, account features.
4. **Third parties involved** — name Google (AdMob, Firebase) and any other SDKs.
5. **Data deletion** — how users can request removal of their data.
6. **Children** — whether the app targets children under 13.

## A minimal template

```markdown
# Privacy Policy — [App Name]

Last updated: [DATE]

[Developer name] ("we") operates [App Name].

## Data we collect
- Device identifiers for advertising (Google AdMob)
- Usage analytics (Google Firebase)

## How we use it
To display advertisements, measure usage and improve the app.

## Third parties
Data may be shared with Google LLC per their privacy policy.

## Contact
Questions or deletion requests: your@email.com
```

<Callout type="warning" title="Match your actual SDKs">
Copy-pasting a generic policy that doesn't mention your real SDKs is a rejection reason. Open your `pubspec.yaml` and list every service that touches user data.
</Callout>

## Where to host it

Options that work with Play Console:

- GitHub Pages / Gist (free, stable URL)
- Your own website (best for branding)
- Firebase Hosting

The URL must load without login and work on mobile.

## Where it appears

1. **Play Console → App content → Privacy policy** — set the URL.
2. **Inside your app** — link it from settings and from any permission dialog.
3. **First-run consent** where required (e.g., GDPR regions).

<Callout type="tip" title="Update when integrations change">
Adding a new SDK? Update the policy the same day. An accurate, current policy is also a trust signal that improves conversion on your listing.
</Callout>

## What's next?

Declare everything officially in the [Data Safety](/docs/play-store/data-safety) form.
