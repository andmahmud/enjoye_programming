## Introduction

The **Data safety** form tells users (and Google) what data your app collects and shares. It is mandatory for every app — and one of the most common sources of rejections when filled carelessly. This page walks through a typical Flutter app using Firebase + AdMob.

## The core question: collected vs shared

- **Collected**: your app or its SDKs transmit data off the device.
- **Shared**: that data goes to third parties *beyond* your direct service providers.

AdMob's advertising ID usage counts as **collected**, and depending on settings, **shared** with Google for ads.

## A typical AdMob + Firebase answer set

| Data type | Collected? | Shared? | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Device/other IDs (advertising ID) | Yes | Yes | Advertising, analytics |
| App interactions (analytics events) | Yes | No* | Analytics |
| Email address (if Firebase Auth) | Yes | No | Account management |
| Crash logs (Crashlytics) | Yes | No | Stability |

\* Firebase Analytics is generally treated as service-provider processing; verify current guidance in Play's help center.

<Callout type="warning" title="Your answers must match reality">
Google cross-checks SDKs found in your binary against your declarations. If you ship AdMob but declare "no data collected", the update gets rejected.
</Callout>

## How to audit your own app

1. Open `pubspec.yaml` — list every analytics/ads/backend package.
2. Check each SDK vendor's "Play data disclosure" documentation (Firebase and AdMob both publish exact recommended answers).
3. Declare **every** type those vendors report.
4. Answer the encryption questions: all HTTPS traffic = data encrypted in transit → yes.

## Encryption & deletion answers

- **Encrypted in transit**: yes if all requests use HTTPS (Firebase and AdMob do).
- **Data deletion request mechanism**: you must offer a way to delete user data — an in-app account deletion flow or a documented email request. Since 2024, apps with account creation **must provide in-app deletion**.

## When you change integrations

Adding Crashlytics next month changes your form. Revisit Data safety during every release checklist item where dependencies changed — it takes five minutes and prevents rejected updates.
