## Introduction

Most rejections are not mysterious — they repeat the same handful of policy themes. This checklist covers what reviewers actually check, especially for ad-monetized Flutter apps.

## The big five policy areas

### 1. Metadata accuracy

- App title ≤ 30 characters, no keyword stuffing ("FREE CAMERA EDITOR PRO 2026 NEW").
- Screenshots must show your real app — mockups of features that don't exist are grounds for removal.
- Icon and feature graphic shouldn't imitate other brands.

### 2. Data transparency

Your [Data Safety](/docs/play-store/data-safety) form, [Privacy Policy](/docs/play-store/privacy-policy) and actual SDKs must tell one consistent story.

### 3. Ads placement policy

Google's **Ads policy** is strict about:

- Ads overlapping or covering UI elements
- Accidental clicks (ads placed near buttons, or shifting content under a finger)
- Interstitials on unexpected moments (launch, back-press spam)
- Ads in notification content

### 4. Functional minimums

Apps must do something meaningful. Thin wrappers around webviews, "wallpaper + 3 images" apps and copycats get removed. Aim for real functionality even if small.

### 5. Account deletion

If users can create accounts, you must offer in-app deletion plus a web link declared in the console.

## Pre-submission checklist

```text
□ versionCode incremented
□ Release build tested on a physical device
□ Privacy policy URL live and current
□ Data safety matches shipped SDKs
□ AdMob test IDs replaced with production IDs
□ No ad overlaps; banners reserve layout space
□ Account deletion flow exists (if auth used)
□ Store listing assets show real screens
```

<Callout type="danger" title="Repeated violations risk account termination">
A single rejected update is normal. Three strikes on the *same* policy can terminate your developer account with all apps. Fix root causes, not just symptoms.
</Callout>

## When your app is rejected

1. Read the exact policy quoted in the email — it links to specifics.
2. Fix the issue completely, don't resubmit hoping for a different reviewer.
3. Reply to the review thread if clarification helps.

Most first-time rejections resolve within one honest revision cycle.
