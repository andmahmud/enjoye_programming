## Introduction

Before writing any ad code, your app must be registered correctly in AdMob. A clean setup — one AdMob app per store listing, well-named ad units — makes earnings reports readable for years.

## Add your app to AdMob

1. Sign in at [apps.admob.com](https://apps.admob.com).
2. Click **Apps → Add app** and choose the platform.
3. If your app is already on Google Play, search for it and link it — this enables better reporting.
4. Note the generated **App ID** (`ca-app-pub-xxxxx~yyyyy`).

The tilde (`~`) marks an App ID; a slash (`/`) marks an Ad Unit ID. Mixing them up is the most common integration bug.

## Link Google Play

In **App settings → App status**, connect your Play Console account. Benefits:

- Automatic verification of ownership
- Combined analytics across both dashboards
- Eligibility for programmatic mediation features

## Plan your ad units

Create one ad unit per placement, not one shared unit:

```text
My Diary App (AdMob app)
├── banner_home        (Banner, adaptive)
├── banner_history     (Banner, adaptive)
├── inter_daily_limit  (Interstitial)
└── rewarded_premium   (Rewarded)
```

Naming convention: `format_location`. When a report says `inter_daily_limit earned $12`, you instantly know what works.

<Callout type="tip" title="You can always add more">
New ad units take a few hours to start serving. Create every placement you plan for before release day so they are warmed up.
</Callout>

## Configure payment details

Under **Payments**, complete your profile and verify identity when prompted. Payments are issued monthly after reaching the threshold (typically $70–$100 depending on country). Do this early — payouts pause until verification is finished.

## App-ads.txt (after publishing)

Once live on Play Store, add your **app-ads.txt** snippet from AdMob to your developer website and reference it. It protects your inventory from spoofing and keeps eCPMs healthy.
