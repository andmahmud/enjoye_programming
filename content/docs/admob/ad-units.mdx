## Introduction

Choosing the right ad format matters more than showing more ads. The wrong format in the wrong place destroys retention; the right format feels natural and pays well. Here is every AdMob format compared.

## Format comparison

| Format | Typical eCPM | Best placement | User impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Banner (adaptive) | Low | Bottom of content screens | Minimal |
| Interstitial | Medium–high | Natural breaks between tasks | High — use sparingly |
| Rewarded video | Highest | User-initiated value exchange | Positive when optional |
| Native | Medium | Inside feeds and lists | Blends with UI |
| App Open | Medium | App launch | Acceptable if instant |

## Banner ads

Small rectangles docked at screen edges. Adaptive banners size themselves to the device and outperform fixed-size banners. Ideal for screens users spend time on — dashboards, history lists.

## Interstitial ads

Full-screen ads shown **between** activities: after finishing a level, between article reads, or on app exit confirmation. Never interrupt mid-task.

## Rewarded ads

Users choose to watch an ad in exchange for value — extra lives, premium features for 24 hours, currency. Because the user consents, rewarded ads deliver the best eCPM *and* the happiest users.

## Native ads

Rendered with your own widgets so they match your design system exactly. More integration work, but perfect inside feeds.

## App Open ads

Shown while your app loads. They monetize launch time without a separate "loading ad" screen.

## A balanced example

A typical utility app earns well without annoying anyone:

- **App Open** on cold start
- One adaptive **banner** on the main dashboard
- An **interstitial** after completing an export/action
- A **rewarded** option to unlock premium for a day

<Callout type="tip" title="Start with two formats">
Launch with banner + rewarded (or banner + interstitial). Measure eCPM per format for two weeks before adding more.
</Callout>
